ZEOSYNC WEBSITE — START HERE

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder (Windows: right-click > Extract All).
2. Open Zeosync-Website/index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Keep all the HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SVG files together in this folder.

Included pages
- index.html: Home
- product.html: Product
- how-it-works.html: How It Works
- pricing.html: Pricing
- about.html: Why Zeosync
- contact.html: Contact / local inquiry brief
- 404.html: Not-found page

No installation, build step, or local server is required. Internal navigation,
styles, scripts, and the favicon use relative local paths.

Internet use
Google Fonts loads online when available; built-in font fallbacks work offline.
Privacy, Terms, and the existing Zeosync contact page are external website links
and require internet access. They are not included as local pages.

Contact and pricing
The contact page prepares a downloadable brief locally; it does not send a lead.
Pricing and the illustrated dashboard are proposals, as labeled on the pages.
Checkout, billing, and live Amazon/Shopify connections are not part of this
marketing website. Confirm pricing and claims before a public launch.

Validation
All internal page links, fragment links, stylesheet/script/favicon references,
and local CSS asset references were checked. JavaScript syntax was checked.
The ZIP was tested for corruption and compared byte-for-byte with its contents.
Browser visual testing was not performed.
