HOW IT WORKS

A better connection
starts with the right setup.

A deliberate start helps keep day-to-day selling simple. Here’s the recommended path from first conversation to your ongoing workflow.

01

CHECK THE FIT

Bring us your real-world setup.

Start with your Amazon marketplace, Shopify store, catalog size, and monthly order volume. Discuss any complicated variants, fulfillment requirements, or recurring issues.

What to confirmSupported marketplace, account requirements, and an appropriate plan.
02

CONNECT & CONFIGURE

Give every product the right match.

Use the supported connection flow, review SKU mappings, and decide which information should move between channels. Keep credentials out of email and inquiry forms.

What to confirmProduct mappings, stock source, supported fields, and sync direction.
03

VERIFY THE WORKFLOW

Start small. Check the details.

Review a limited group of products and orders before expanding. Check inventory changes, order details, fulfillment handling, and the return information that matters to your team.

What to confirmExpected results for your own catalog and fulfillment process.
04

RUN & REFINE

Make the workflow part of your day.

Use a regular review routine to check orders and returns. Revisit settings as your catalog, order volume, or selling regions change.

What to confirmWho checks issues, how to get help, and when your plan needs to grow.
