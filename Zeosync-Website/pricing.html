KNOW WHAT YOU’RE CHOOSING

No fine-print guessing game.

What counts as a synced order?

Under these proposed plans, one unique order imported from Amazon counts once. Updating that same order does not add another order to your monthly count.

What counts as an active SKU?

A distinct product variant enabled for synchronization counts as an active SKU. Your catalog and mapping requirements should be reviewed before selecting a plan.

Are there percentage-of-sales fees?

These proposed Zeosync plans use a fixed monthly subscription, with no percentage-of-sales fee. Amazon, Shopify, fulfillment, taxes, and other third-party charges are separate.

What happens if I exceed my plan?

The proposed model does not add automatic overage charges. Contact Zeosync to agree an upgrade or custom scope. How syncing is handled at the limit must be confirmed before activation.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

The proposed offer is month-to-month. Changes, cancellation timing, and any applicable refund terms will be confirmed in the service agreement before billing begins.

Is every Amazon marketplace supported?

Do not assume universal coverage. Confirm your selling region, seller account, fulfillment model, and required return actions before connecting.

Why choose Zeosync over another connector?

Choose the service that fits your operation. Zeosync’s focus is a clear Amazon–Shopify workflow with understandable plans. Compare catalog support, onboarding, order handling, and help with issues—not just the headline price.
