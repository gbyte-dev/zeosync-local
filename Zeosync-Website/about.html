WHY ZEOSYNC

Selling on two channels
shouldn’t feel like two jobs.

Zeosync is built around a straightforward idea: connected commerce should make the work of selling easier to manage.

A sale is the beginning.
Everything after it
needs to stay connected.

A product changes. Stock moves. An order arrives. A customer needs to return something. Each detail matters—and each one becomes harder to follow when your channels work separately.

Zeosync’s purpose is to bring the essential Amazon and Shopify workflows together, so merchants can spend less time piecing the picture back together.

That means staying focused on useful connections, understandable pricing, and the practical details of your store.
